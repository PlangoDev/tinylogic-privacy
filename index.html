1. Who we are

Tiny Logic is developed and published by Mazell Smack-Dubose, an individual developer based in Delaware, United States ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and the UK GDPR together with the UK Data Protection Act 2018, we are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we are not required to do so. Contact details are in section 18.

2. Scope

This policy covers: (a) the Tiny Logic game and its free demo on all platforms ("the Game"); (b) the Tiny Logic Discord server at discord.gg/tinylogic and its bot ("the Community"); and (c) this website. It does not cover third-party services you use to obtain or play the Game (such as itch.io, Steam or Discord), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Children and age of consent

On first launch the Game asks whether you are old enough to consent to optional data collection. The age that applies is determined from your device's language and region settings, not from any location lookup:

RegionMinimum age to consent
United States13 (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
United Kingdom, European Union, European Economic Area, and all other or unknown regions16 (GDPR Article 8 and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code; we apply the highest age used by any EU member state)

Your answer is stored only on your device. If you indicate that you are below the applicable age, the Game does not request consent and collects and transmits no analytics or crash data; all gameplay features remain available. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below these ages. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly. The Community is subject to Discord's Terms of Service, which require users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires).

4. Data we collect

4.1 Game analytics (only with your consent)

If you are old enough and select Share anonymous stats, the Game sends the following, linked to a random installation identifier generated on your device:

CategoryExamples
Device and technical dataOperating system and version, CPU model and core count, memory, graphics hardware, screen and window size, display count and refresh rate, UI scale, presence of touch input, Linux display server, game version and language.
Usage dataSession start, length and time of day; days since installation; time spent in game modes; lessons started, steps reached, hints used, quiz answers (correct or incorrect), lessons completed or exited; types of parts placed and how they were found; chips made; programs loaded or assembled; projects created, opened and saved (counts only); settings changed; features first used; update notices opened.
Performance dataFrame rate, slow frames, simulation speed, compile times, memory use, and the size of the current board (counts of parts, wires and transistors).
Optional questionnaireAge range, occupation or field of study, how you found the Game, and what you want to build. Every question may be skipped.
Linked account (optional)Your itch.io username, only if you separately choose to link it.
Network dataLike any internet service, our analytics provider receives the IP address from which data is sent. We instruct it not to derive location from that address, and we do not use IP addresses to identify you.

4.2 Crash reports (only with your consent)

After a crash, and only if you are old enough, the Game asks whether to send a crash report. A report contains the type of error, the game version, the operating system, and a technical trace of where the Game's code stopped. File paths are reduced to file names so that your system user name is not included. Reports never include your projects or anything you typed.

4.3 Update check (no personal data)

On start-up the Game requests a small file (version.txt) from this website to check for updates. No identifier or personal data is sent. Our host, GitHub, necessarily receives your IP address as part of serving the file; see GitHub's privacy statement.

4.4 Community data

See section 13.

4.5 Communications

If you contact us by email, Discord or social media, we process the content of your message and your contact details in order to respond.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your real name, email address or payment details through the Game; the contents or names of your projects, circuits, chips or programs; anything you type, including search terms, notes and code; file or folder names on your device; your precise or approximate location; contacts, camera, microphone or files outside the Game. We do not use advertising identifiers, do not show advertising, and do not build advertising profiles.

8. International transfers

We and our providers are located in the United States. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, your personal data is transferred to the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension where the recipient is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum) incorporated in the providers' data processing terms, together with appropriate supplementary measures.

9. Retention

  • Analytics and crash reports: kept for no longer than 24 months from collection, then deleted or fully anonymised, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Data waiting on your device: deleted from your device as soon as it is sent, or immediately if you turn stats off.
  • Community moderation records: kept for up to 12 months, or longer only where needed to handle an ongoing safety issue.
  • Purchase verification records: kept while the associated role exists, to prevent the same key being used twice.
  • Correspondence: kept for as long as needed to deal with your message, and no longer than 24 months afterwards.

10. Your rights (EU, EEA and UK)

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to: access your personal data (Art. 15); rectification (Art. 16); erasure (Art. 17); restriction of processing (Art. 18); data portability (Art. 20); object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21); and withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement, or in the UK with the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

11. Your rights (United States)

California. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), California residents may request to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collected, the sources, purposes and recipients; to delete it; and to correct inaccurate information. The categories we collect, only with consent, are identifiers (a random installation ID and, if linked, an itch.io username; IP address as received), internet or other electronic network activity (usage and performance data), and inferences we do not draw for profiling. We do not sell or "share" personal information (as those terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA), do not use or disclose sensitive personal information, and have not done so in the preceding 12 months. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

Other states. Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Oregon, Texas, Utah and Virginia) may have rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of their personal data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and profiling. We do not engage in targeted advertising, sale or profiling. You may appeal any decision on your request by replying to our response; if your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Because the Game collects nothing without opt-in consent, no additional opt-out is required. We honour any request to stop processing however it is made.

12. How to exercise your rights

The fastest way is in the Game: Settings > Stats > off stops all analytics and crash reports immediately and deletes anything still waiting on your device; Settings > itch.io link > off stops sending your username. For access, deletion, correction or portability, email dubosemazell90@gmail.com. To locate your data, include your installation ID, found as installId in Tiny Logic's settings.json (macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Mazell/TinyLogic/; Windows: %APPDATA%\Mazell\TinyLogic\; Linux: ~/.local/share/Mazell/TinyLogic/). If you cannot find it, write anyway and tell us roughly when you played. We will respond within one month (GDPR / UK GDPR) or 45 days (US state laws), extendable where the law allows, and may need to verify your request. You may use an authorised agent where the law permits.

13. Discord server, bot and Rich Presence

Rich Presence. If the Discord desktop application is running, the Game may display your current activity on your Discord profile (for example "Building in sandbox" and a transistor count; never a project's name). The Game communicates only with the Discord application on your own device. Whether this is visible, and to whom, is controlled by your Discord Activity Privacy settings.

Community and bot. In the Community, our bot processes: your Discord user ID, username and server roles; the content of messages you post, for automated moderation (detecting hate speech, harassment and spam, which may be analysed by Google Cloud's content moderation service) and for activity levels; bug reports, ideas, builds, votes and attachments you submit through its commands; and an itch.io download key you choose to submit with /verify, which is checked with itch.io solely to confirm your purchase and grant a role. Moderation actions and their reasons are logged for our moderators. We may review aggregated, de-identified Community activity to improve the Game. Message content is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.

14. Purchases

Purchases are processed by itch.io (and, in future, Steam), which act as independent controllers of your payment and account information. We receive from itch.io limited purchase records (such as the purchase date, amount, country, and the name and email address you provided to itch.io) in order to fulfil orders, provide Steam keys to existing buyers, process refunds and comply with tax and accounting obligations. We never receive your full payment card details.

15. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to analytics, moderation logs and purchase records is restricted to the developer. No system is completely secure; if a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where required and inform affected users without undue delay.

16. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. Automated moderation in the Community may remove a message or apply a temporary timeout; you may ask a human moderator to review any such action.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gains new features. The effective date above will change, and material changes will be announced in the Game's update notes and the Community. Where a change requires new consent, the Game will ask again before collecting any new data.
