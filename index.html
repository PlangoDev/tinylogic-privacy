We do not sell your data, do not show advertising, and do not build advertising profiles. You can turn collection off at any time in Settings. 1. Who we are

Tiny Logic is developed and published by Mazell Smack-Dubose, an individual developer resident in the State of Delaware, United States ("we", "us", "our"). We determine the purposes and means of the processing described in this policy and are therefore the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and the UK GDPR, and the business or controller under applicable United States privacy laws. Given the nature and scale of our processing, we are not required to appoint a data protection officer. Contact details are in section 30.

2. Scope and acceptance

This policy applies to: (a) the Tiny Logic software, including the full game, the free demo and any future editions, on every platform ("the Game"); (b) the Tiny Logic Discord server and its bot ("the Community"); (c) the Tiny Logic websites, including this site and the update service ("the Sites"); and (d) your communications with us. It does not apply to third-party services you use to buy, download or play the Game, such as itch.io, Steam or Discord, which have their own privacy policies.

This policy explains how we handle personal data. It is not itself a request for consent. Where we rely on consent, we ask for it separately and specifically inside the Game, as described in section 6.

On first launch, the Game asks whether you meet the minimum age to consent to optional data collection. The applicable age is determined from your device's language and region settings, not by locating you:

6. How consent works

  • Opt-in only. Analytics are off by default. Nothing in section 7 that requires consent is collected until you choose Share anonymous stats.
  • Equal choice. Share anonymous stats and No thanks are presented with equal prominence, and refusing changes nothing about the Game.
  • Granular. Analytics, crash reports and itch.io account linking are separate choices. Crash reports are requested individually after each crash (Yes, No or Always).
  • Informed. The consent screen summarises what is collected and links to this policy.
  • Withdrawable. You may withdraw at any time in Settings, as easily as you gave consent. Withdrawal stops collection immediately and deletes any data still waiting on your device. It does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Re-asked when needed. If we introduce a new category of data that falls outside section 7, the Game will ask for your consent again before collecting it.

8. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your real name, email address, postal address or payment details through the Game; the names or contents of your projects, circuits, chips or programs; text you type, including search terms, notes and code (only the length of a search may be recorded); names of files or folders on your device; your precise or approximate location; your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any files outside the Game; browsing history; advertising identifiers. We do not use cookies or tracking pixels on the Sites.

9. Purposes of processing

  1. Improving the Game: understanding which features, lessons and content are used, enjoyed, confusing or abandoned, and deciding what to build, change or fix.
  2. Quality and stability: detecting, diagnosing and fixing crashes, bugs and errors.
  3. Performance: measuring and improving speed and stability across the hardware our players use.
  4. Education design: improving the effectiveness of lessons, hints and quizzes.
  5. Service operation: delivering updates, update notices and the Game's online features.
  6. Community: operating, moderating and keeping the Community safe, and verifying purchases for community roles.
  7. Customer support: responding to your messages, bug reports and requests.
  8. Business analytics: understanding the size and composition of our audience (in aggregate) to plan development, platforms and translations.
  9. Aggregate reporting: publishing aggregate, non-identifying statistics about the Game and its community (for example in update notes, store pages or promotional material).
  10. Security and integrity: protecting the Game, the demo and our services from tampering, fraud and abuse (see section 13).
  11. Legal compliance: meeting tax, accounting and legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.

We do not use personal data for advertising, sale to third parties, or training artificial intelligence models offered to others.

11. Pseudonymous, de-identified and aggregate data

Game analytics are pseudonymous: they are linked to your installation ID, not to your name or contact details. We do not hold any information that lets us connect an installation ID to a real person unless you choose to link an itch.io account or provide your ID to us. Because of this:

  • In accordance with GDPR Article 11 and equivalent provisions, we are not able to identify you from our analytics on our own. To exercise rights over analytics data, you must provide information that lets us locate it: your installation ID, or the itch.io username you linked (see section 21). Without one of these, we cannot find, and therefore cannot act on, your analytics data.
  • We may create de-identified and aggregate data from analytics. Such data is no longer personal data. We may retain it indefinitely, use it for any lawful purpose, and publish it, and we commit not to attempt to re-identify it.

12. Future features and versions

The Game is in active development and receives frequent updates. This policy is written so that it applies to new features, modes, content and editions as they are added, as long as the data involved falls within the categories in section 7 and is used for the purposes in section 9. For example, new lessons, parts, tools, challenges or game modes generate gameplay and interaction data (category C) and performance data (category D) covered by your existing consent.

If a future feature requires a new category of personal data or a new purpose not described here (for example user accounts, cloud saves, online multiplayer, shared online content, voice, or payment within the Game), we will update this policy before launching it and, where consent is the legal basis, the Game will ask for your consent before that data is collected.

13. Demo edition and integrity checks

The free demo collects the same categories, under the same consent, as the full Game, with an indication that the data comes from the demo. To protect the Game, the demo may check on launch whether its own files have been modified. If analytics consent has been given, the result of that check (which type of modification, the demo version and operating system, and how far you have played) may be sent once. No file contents, file paths or personal files are read or sent. These checks never affect the operation of the demo or your device, and no action is taken against any player as a result. If a player who used the demo later installs the full Game, the full Game may send, with consent, a single indication that it was upgraded from the demo.

14. Storage on your device

The Game stores files on your device that are necessary to function, including settings, save files, your consent choices and age answer, the installation ID, logs, caches, and analytics waiting to be sent. These remain on your device under your control and can be deleted by uninstalling the Game and removing its data folder:

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Mazell/TinyLogic/
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Mazell\TinyLogic\
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/Mazell/TinyLogic/

The Sites do not use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for tracking.

22. Discord community, bot and Rich Presence

22.1 Rich Presence

If the Discord desktop application is running, the Game may display your current activity on your Discord profile (for example "Building in sandbox" and a transistor count; never a project's name). The Game communicates only with the Discord application on your own device. Visibility is controlled by your Discord Activity Privacy settings.

22.2 Community and bot

When you participate in the Community, our bot processes: your Discord user ID, username, display name and roles; the content of messages you post and attachments you share; your submissions through bot commands (bug reports, ideas, builds, votes and their attachments, including any speech transcribed from submitted videos); activity used for community levels; and any itch.io download key you choose to submit with /verify, which is checked with itch.io solely to confirm a purchase and assign a role. Messages are analysed automatically to detect hate speech, harassment and spam, which may involve Google Cloud's content moderation service. Moderation actions are logged for moderators. Submitted bug reports and ideas may be used to improve the Game and may be displayed publicly in the Community. We may analyse Community activity in aggregate or de-identified form to improve the Game. Message content is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not used to train AI models offered to others.

23. Purchases and Steam keys

Purchases are processed by itch.io and, when available, Steam, which act as independent controllers of your payment and account information. We receive limited purchase records from them (such as the date, amount, currency, country, and the name and email address you gave the store) to fulfil orders, deliver Steam keys to existing buyers, handle refunds and support, prevent fraud, and meet tax and accounting obligations. We never receive your full payment card details. If you contact us to receive a Steam key, we use the details you provide only to verify your purchase and deliver the key.

24. Security and breach notification

We protect personal data with measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit (TLS), restricted access limited to the developer, minimisation of the data collected, separation of identifiers from player identity, and the use of reputable providers bound by data processing agreements. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where required, notify affected individuals without undue delay where required by law, and take steps to contain it.

25. Automated processing

We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing, and we do not profile players for such decisions. Automated moderation in the Community may remove a message or apply a temporary timeout; you may request human review of any such action by contacting a moderator or us.

26. Disclosures required by law

We may disclose personal data if we believe in good faith that it is necessary to comply with a law, regulation, court order or valid legal process; to respond to a lawful request from public authorities; to protect the rights, property or safety of players, the public or us; or to detect, prevent or address fraud, security or technical issues. Where permitted, we will notify you of such a request.

27. Business transfers

If Tiny Logic or its assets are transferred to another entity (for example through the formation of a company owned by the developer, a merger, acquisition or sale), personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. The recipient will be bound by this policy or a policy providing at least equivalent protection, and we will notify players of any material change in who is responsible for their data.

28. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Game, our practices or the law. The effective date and version number at the top will change. Material changes will be announced in the Game's update notes and in the Community before they take effect.
